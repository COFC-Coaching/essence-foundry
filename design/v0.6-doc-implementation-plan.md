# Essence v0.6 → Foundry: implementation plan

Source of truth: the "Essence System v0.6" Google Doc (Drive id
`1NNtL80aa3PRPRZ09O_MhYk3xh6t-WlzuuPMog5uiQCk`), exported to text on 2026-09-26. `L1234` means
line 1234 of that export. Every line of the Doc (lines 1–8458, all Parts and Appendices A–F) was
read and compared with the code at `claude/elastic-lamarr-32f578`. The website markdown and the
change document were not used as sources for this plan.

Status of the working tree: a partial first pass is uncommitted:
- Character `tier` is relabeled Team Tier.
- `baseCombatDice = 6 + poolBonus`.
- `level` = AP earned, with an `advancementPointsSpent` counter.
- Tier and Level are removed from the enemy headers and wizards.
- Monster Resilience defaults to the old Tier 1 value.

Those edits agree with the Doc, except for how Team Tier is stored (Decision 1). They will be
revised to fit this plan, not shipped as they are.

## What does NOT change

The Doc keeps these as they are in the code today. The plan does not touch them.

- **Reach is per character.** "Reach measures how much accumulated ordinary Influence pressure a
  character can absorb… It begins at 1… A Team Tier increase changes the scale of meaningful costs,
  not the character's Reach rating" (L1903). The following are all unchanged:
  - `system.reach` and `reachPressure`.
  - The pressure order: Reach, then Temporary Influence, then Core Influence.
  - Influence Breach skipping Reach.
  - Reach Triggers, and one character paying another's cost.
  - Pressure resetting at the Adventure boundary.
- **Temporary and Core Influence:** a running total, five Core spaces, and Consequence Cards.
  Consequence Card text matches the Doc.
- **Attribute, Resource and Defense formulas.** Stamina, Focus and Mana are 2 + the two highest
  Attributes in the domain; Defenses are 2 + the two lowest. Creation gives 7 points, max 3.
- **Combat Styles and Expertises.** Styles get 5 points, max Rank 2 at creation. 4 Expertises;
  the limit is Rank, +1 for the Distinction's Style. 10 learned cards, plus Mastery.
- **Equipment capacity.** Armory 8 includes Inventory 4. A complete item costs 1, a loose
  Chassis or Fitting ½, an Augment 0. Temporary Equipment costs 0. The used/unused allocation
  flag stays.
- **Damage and Wounds.**
  - Resilience accumulates between Turn starts.
  - Breach skips Resilience.
  - Resistance and Vulnerability are ±2, cancel each other, and don't stack.
  - Temporary Wounds absorb Wounds first.
  - Core spaces fill Light, Light, Serious, Serious, Critical, earliest empty first, with Wound
    State from the highest severity.
  - The nine fallback Wound Cards and their numbers are unchanged.
- **Death Track.** The states are none, dying and stabilized. Dying starts at 0 when the fifth
  space fills and advances at each Turn start. The threshold is 5, or 7 for Deathless. Acting while
  Dying is unchanged; so are extra Wounds while the track is full, and −1 per Recovery when not
  Dying.
- **Recovery.** Resources restore 25% of max, rounded up, then Anima points. Recovery also clears
  Strain and restores the Adaptability reroll.
- **Unopposed rolls, Mastery, cooldowns** (per Round and per Encounter), **Perform Task**, and
  **Reconfigure** (burn 3).

## Decisions (answered 2026-09-26)

- **Team Tier:** stored on a new `team` Actor type, which holds Team Tier and the Team record
  (option 1a). Characters link to a Team, and raising the Tier clears every member's Temporary
  Influence. Decreases are blocked, since the Doc says Tier only increases.
- **Adventure reset:** stops refilling Consumable Kits and Function Augments.
- **Dilettante:** removed from the compendium. Characters who own it keep their copy.
- **Damage-type Conditions (Bleeding etc.):** kept. Not changed.
- **Unaffordable cards:** keep the warning. Not blocked.
- **Critical Wound recovery:** use the Doc text, "four weeks (28 qualifying days)", marked Playtest.
- **Linked Mount Link:** changing a Link is part of Reconfigure (3 burned dice).
- **Calling profiles:** keep all 8, labeled as Campaign-supplied playtest profiles.
- **Stabilize "applicable modifiers":** still undefined. Not built.

## Decisions needed before building (original questions)

1. **Where Team Tier lives.** The Doc treats Team Tier as one group value on a Team record:
   - "Team Tier belongs on the Team record" (L764).
   - "Tiers 1 through 5… only increases" (L2259, L2283).
   - "When Team Tier increases, clear every character's remaining Temporary Influence" (L2287).
   - Appendix F lists a Team sheet (L8257).

   You chose a per-actor field before any of this had been read. The options are:
   - **(a) A `team` Actor type** (recommended). It holds Team Tier and the Team record:
     - Identity, Major Goal, Methods, Principles.
     - Things and Commitments, tagged Asset, Obligation, Borrowed or Pledged.
     - Organizations, as Friendly, Neutral or Hostile.

     Characters link to a Team. Raising the Team's Tier clears every member's Temporary Influence
     in one step. This is Foundry-native, and it supports several parties in one world.
   - **(b) A world setting.** One Team Tier per world, with no record.
   - **(c) Keep it per character,** accepting that members' copies can drift apart and that each
     increase clears only one character's Temporary Influence.

2. **Enemy engine.** Mooks and Normals get printed allowances, per your instruction to follow the
   Doc (Phase 4). No decision is needed on the direction, but the Doc gaps listed at the end need
   answers from Ryan.

3. **Behaviour changes that live tables will notice.** Each needs a yes or no:
   - **Adventure reset.** "Reset All for New Adventure" currently refills every Consumable Kit and
     Function Augment. The Doc says Adventure end does not replenish consumables (L2556, L1483).
     It also says each Function Augment states its own recovery (L1548). Stop auto-refilling?
   - **Extra conditions.** Ten Condition items ship that are not in the Doc: Bleeding, Chilled,
     Concussed, Corroded, Displaced, Distorted, Punctured, Revealed, Shocked and Withered. The
     Doc says to "apply only named Conditions" and "do not infer Conditions from… Damage type"
     (L4136). Remove them from the compendium? Copies already on actors stay.
   - **Dilettante.** It is not in the Doc (nine Distinctions, L5578). Remove it from the
     compendium? Characters who already have it keep the item and its +5 Resources until a GM
     changes it.
   - **Calling.** The Doc says numeric Manifestation profiles "are not included in this edition"
     (L6415, L6457). Foundry ships 8 authored profiles. Keep them, labeled as Campaign-supplied
     playtest profiles?
   - **Unaffordable cards.** The Doc says a card "cannot be played" when its costs can't be paid
     (L3647). The system currently warns and plays anyway, per this project's "warn, never block"
     convention. Block, or keep the warning?

4. **Open items the Doc now answers.** Your brief listed these as pending Ryan; the Doc text now
   says something about each. Confirm whether to use the Doc text or keep waiting:
   - **Team Tier decrease.** The Doc says Tier "only increases" (L2283), so there is nothing to
     clear. Recommend blocking decreases with a warning.
   - **Critical Wound recovery.** "a Critical Wound clears after four weeks… twenty-eight
     qualifying days" (L4027, also L3982). It is labeled Playtest.
   - **Linked Mount Link.** Reconfigure's text includes "establish, change, or end one permitted
     Linked Mount Link" (L4259), so it would cost 3 burned dice. The Linked Mounts section
     (L1560) still has a draft note.
   - **Stabilize "applicable modifiers".** Still undefined (L4277). Not building it.

## Phase 1: Team Tier, pools, Advancement (data model and migration)

**Done in 0.8.0** (uncommitted): 1.1 to 1.5 and 1.7 in full. 1.6 in part: the floors of 0 and the
Resource clamp are applied to derived values only. Two parts of 1.6 are not done:
- The caps of 5 on Attributes, Styles and Skills are not enforced.
- Attributes are not floored at 1.

Either one would clamp stored values in live worlds, so each needs its own decision.

1.1 **Team Tier.** Built per Decision 1:
- Range 1–5; increases only (L2259, L2283).
- An increase clears every character's Temporary Influence (L2287). It refunds nothing, doesn't
  touch Core Influence, doesn't reset Reach pressure, and grants no personal stats.

1.2 **Pools.** `baseCombatDice = 6 + poolBonus`, one shared base for both pools (L3455, L4890).
This is already in the working tree. Also:
- Manifestations share the caller's base.
- Elites use the base printed in their profile (see 4.x).

1.3 **Advancement Points.**
- `level` = total AP earned; a new character starts at 1; no cap (L4715, L4725).
- `advancementPointsSpent` is a manual counter; unspent = Level − spent. Already in the working
  tree.
- Flag overspending.
- No Skill Tree content: node rewards are in playtest (L4687, L4756).

1.4 **Equipment access by Team Tier.**
- Replace the Reach-vs-`cost` warning with a per-Component check: each Chassis and Fitting Tier
  must be at most Team Tier (L1793). "A complete modular item has no combined Tier."
- The check stays a soft warning. Obtained gear is always usable (L1795, L1809).
- Stop showing `cost` as a "Reach requirement". The field stays in the schema for stored items.
- Reach itself is unchanged.

1.5 **Grant features rewritten to the Doc.**
- **Quartermaster's Due:** a Chassis plus a compatible Fitting, each up to Team Tier + 1, max 5
  (L1829).
- **Internal Compartment:** one small item of ordinary utility equipment within the Team's
  ordinary access (L5405).
- **Inherited Tools and Integrated Tool:** use no Armory or Inventory capacity.
- **Prepared Cache:** stops being a +1 Reach Trigger; its extra uses cost ordinary pressure.
- **Letters of Standing:** extra uses cost ordinary pressure through Reach, not Breach (L5486).

1.6 **Caps and floors** (L982, L4727, L4852).
- Attributes range 1–5.
- Combat Styles and Non-Combat Skills range 0–5.
- Movement, Resilience, Defenses and Resources floor at 0.
- The current Resource value clamps when its max falls, and stays put when its max rises.

1.7 **Migration.**
- Every schema change ships with a `migrateData` / `migration.mjs` case and a
  `test-migration.mjs` case.
- No value renames without an alias.
- A one-time GM notice lists characters who were above Tier 1. Their pools become 6 and their
  Level becomes "AP earned"; nothing is converted automatically, per your earlier choice.

## Phase 3: Combat procedures (Part VII, Appendix B)

**Done in 0.10.0:** 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.9, 3.10, 3.11 (enemies, and opening a space), 3.12,
3.14, 3.16 and 3.17 (except Cover's concealment line, which the Doc export does not carry).
**Done in 0.10.1:** 3.6, 3.7, 3.8, 3.13, 3.15, and 3.11's nonlethal-unconscious for characters.
Phase 3 is complete except Cover's concealment line (not in the Doc export).
Notes: Stabilize's "applicable modifiers" stays undefined (Decision list), so the card asks only
whether treatment requirements are met. Prepare Action's reaction-chain ordering is table-run; the
sheet only pays and rolls.

3.1 **Initiative.**
- Commit 1 up to the base Action Pool, with no 0 "pass" (L3380).
- PCs win ties against enemies (L3403), via Foundry's `_sortCombatants`.
- Mooks and Normals: see Phase 4.

3.2 **Turn structure** (L3444, L3461), on Foundry's `_onStartTurn` / `_onEndTurn`.
- **Start of Turn:**
  - Expire any Prepare Action and its reserved dice.
  - Resolve Dazed: burn 3 Action dice (or all remaining if fewer), then remove it.
- **End of Turn:**
  - Prompt for upkeep, such as Gestalt Adaptation Stamina and Unstable.
  - Reaction Pool = base + unused, **unreserved** Action dice.

3.3 **Burned vs rolled dice.**
- A card-level "burn N, no roll" shape for Dash, Stabilize, Reconfigure and the Species cards.
- Burned dice never count toward a card's minimum (L3639).

3.4 **Prepare Action** (new Basic card, L4321–4343).
- Burn 2 and reserve X dice for a chosen Action and trigger.
- Fire it from the reserved dice as the one response in a chain.
- It expires at the next Turn start and stays out of the Reaction Pool.
- Enemies and Full Manifestations need GM permission.

3.5 **Stabilize** (L4277–4285).
- Burn 3; target yourself or an adjacent full-track target.
- It actually sets the state to Stabilized, before the Acting-While-Dying check.
- It can also target a Defeated simplified enemy.

3.6 **Damage components** (L3897–3919). Rework Apply Damage:
- Enter a list of components (amount + Damage type).
- The type decides the domain (8 Physical, Psychic/Arcane Mental, Radiant/Necrotic Spiritual).
- Apply the generic flat reduction once to the total, taken from the largest component, with the
  target choosing on ties.
- Then resolve each component in order: Resistance/Vulnerability, Resilience or Breach,
  Temporary Wounds, then Core Wounds.
- This replaces the old domain-cycling rule. It also fixes type/domain mismatches such as
  "Physical Psychic".

3.7 **Weakened.** Implement it as a −1 source of the 3.6 flat reduction (L4189).

3.8 **Nonlethal.** A per-component checkbox (L4116). If it fills the last space:
- A PC becomes Stabilized and unconscious.
- A simplified enemy becomes Defeated but stable.

3.9 **Death is final** (L4060). Add a `dead` state to `deathTrackState` (an additive choice, so
no migration). Reaching the threshold:
- Sets `dead`.
- Applies Foundry's own defeated/dead status.
- Locks out every automatic transition afterwards.

3.10 **Death Track edges.**
- A refilled track resumes from the recorded step while the Critical Wound remains (L4100).
- Removing the Critical Wound resets the step to 0, even when the state is already `none`
  (L4100, L2730).

3.11 **Unconscious.**
- Apply Foundry's status.
- A Defeated simplified enemy is always unconscious (L4062).
- Opening a Wound space ends it.

3.12 **Fatigued** (new ordinary Condition, L4138–4144, L2702).
- Grant Recovery halves each base amount (round up) before adding Anima.
- Offer "remove Fatigued: causes remedied" afterwards.

3.13 **Natural Wound recovery** (L2720, L3982).
- Grant Recovery lets the GM pick which Wounds clear (Light at a Recovery, Serious at Downtime).
- The lowest-first order is kept only for active healing.

3.14 **Encounter boundary** (L4667, L3040).
- "New Encounter" clears Combo, Lock, Threads, Authority, Stance and Unstable, and returns a
  Full Manifestation.
- Deleting the Combat document stops clearing Threads and Authority.

3.15 **Concentration** (L4629). On a Core Wound, prompt: burn 1 available die, or end
Concentration.

3.16 **Smaller items.**
- Helpless targets use the unopposed roll (L4531).
- The unaware tax can't be skipped by committing every die.
- Equipment Cards use their printed minimum (L4205).
- Recover Influence can clear a chosen card, not only the last one.

3.17 **Condition text brought to the Doc.**
- Blinded, Dazed, Prone and Weakened.
- The specialty Conditions: Exposed (−1 all Defenses, which also feeds the Defense calculation),
  Rallied, Strain, Stance, Unstable, Broken, Possessed, Lock (detection, not line of sight) and
  Concentration.
- Cover's concealment line.
- Basic Melee and Ranged Attack targeting objects, Helpless targets, and suspected spaces.

## Phase 4: Enemies (Part XIV and Appendix C, following the Doc)

**Done in 0.11.0:** 4.1 through 4.7. Notes: the reduced engine's Dazed waiver and unaware tax
waiver hold because those steps only run in the full-engine branch of `_onStartTurn`. Defeated
(4.4) shipped in 0.10.0/0.10.1. Enemy Senses stay the free-text field the sheet already had.

4.1 **Engine split** (L6867).
- **Mooks and Normals:**
  - Printed Actions per Turn (1 or 2) and Reactions per Round.
  - Initiative is a 2d10 roll with no Action cost.
  - Task Dice (4 or 5); abilities roll their printed dice.
  - No Pools and no Stamina/Focus/Mana.
  - Dazed and the unaware tax are waived.
  - One utility Basic per Turn, using an Action.
- **Elites:** printed base Pool (6 in the examples), printed Roll Limit, and Resources only when
  an ability spends them.

4.2 **Enemy data model.**
- New fields: `initiativeDice`, `actionsPerTurn`, `reactionsPerRound`, `taskDice`, `rollLimit`.
- Abilities gain `kind` (action/reaction/triggered) and `dice`.
- Printed Defenses become directly settable, because enemy Defenses are printed, not derived.
- Additive fields only.

4.3 **Limited uses** (L6922).
- Rename frequency `perCombat` to "between Recoveries".
- The old value maps through `LEGACY_VALUE_ALIASES`, with a test.
- Uses stop refilling at Round 1 of each Combat.
- Add an enemy "Recovery" action that refreshes them.
- Reaction allowances refresh at the start of each Round (L6879).

4.4 **Defeated** (L6910–6916).
- Always unconscious, with a dying/stable flag.
- Stabilize and nonlethal can make it stable.
- Opening a Wound space ends the incapacity.

4.5 **The sheet shows only what an enemy uses.** A compact Mook/Normal layout:
- Class, Role, Wounds, Defenses, Resilience, Movement and Senses.
- Actions, Reactions and limited abilities (L7131).

4.6 **Budgets and wizard.**
- Defaults match the Starting Enemy Examples: Resilience 0 / 1–2 / 2–3, and no Temporary Wounds.
- The wizard never picks Basic Melee, Basic Ranged or Defend (L6869).

4.7 **Content.** A compendium of the ten Starting Enemy Examples (L7188–7376).

## Phase 5: Combat Styles (Part X, Appendix E)

**Done in 0.12.0:** every bullet below. Notes: Lock's "detection lost by end of Turn" and a
Contingency's Trigger are table-judged clicks. Collapse resolves the filling component, then posts
any later components for the GM to recheck against the returned caller (the actor swap makes a
fully automatic recheck unreliable). Possessed severing (3 Action dice by the subject) is text on
the Rites tooltip, not a button, since the subject's sheet has no record of the link.

- **Magecraft:** duplicate Threads allowed; effect text brought to full Doc wording.
- **Leadership:** Authority stored per card; Orator holds 2 results per card, and spending one
  keeps the other.
- **Ritualism:** Rite cap of 3, +1 with Invoker's Final Echo. Possessed: the newest same-name
  effect replaces the old one; an unwilling subject can sever with 3 dice.
- **Ballistics:** Marksman can hold 2 Locks; Lock ends when detection is lost.
- **Cunning:** Strategist can hold 2 Contingencies; firing is optional; one trigger per Round.
- **Gestalt:** Efficient Transformation minimum 0. Off-turn assumption means the first upkeep is
  at the end of your next Turn.
- **Psionics:** forced Strain above 6 deals Breach Psychic Damage; burning 2 Action dice removes
  2 Strain.
- **Calling:**
  - The entry cost table (3/3/3/4/4/5 extra dice, plus Mana equal to Rank). Summoner reduces
    only the dice.
  - One shared five-space Manifestation Wound track.
  - Broken is character-wide and ends when a Manifestation Wound is removed.
  - Collapse discards the surplus and gives the caller 1 direct Spiritual Core Wound.
  - Return costs 1 die, and you must return before changing forms.
  - The form's Conditions end on return.
  - The Rank 2 native Reaction minimum is 2.
