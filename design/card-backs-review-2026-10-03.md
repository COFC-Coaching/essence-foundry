# Combat Card backs: review draft (2026-10-03)

**342 of 396 cards** have a back.
Every ranked Action and Reaction in the playtest catalogue, with the Non-Combat face written for it. Basic and Species cards are excluded. A card listed under *Single-sided* got no back on purpose. Mark anything to change; nothing is committed yet.

Rule: examples give the scope of power for the card's own Rank; the optional second line names the next rung up. Rank 0 is one small thing within reach, Rank 1 is one person-sized thing or one room with real force, Rank 2 is several things or an area, a cart, a vault door.

## Ballistics (31 backs, 13 single-sided)

*Rank 1 reaction, Hex* · front: Trigger: The enemy imposes an ordinary magical Condition on you. Response: On success, suppress that Condition on yourself until the start of your next turn and give the enemy -1 Harmony for the same duration.

When a charm settles on you, shrug it off and send it home: the glamour fades, the sleep does not take, the fear lifts, and the one who sent it goes pale and shaky for the scene.  
Not enough to break the working for good or protect anyone but yourself.
