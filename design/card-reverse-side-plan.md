# Combat Card reverse side: out-of-combat use

Source: "Essence System v0.7" Google Doc (1ddCbiCyfaelCabY9SQmigpII6WePiOUf6EXKjLCvr18), read 2026-10-03,
plus Shane and Ryan's design call (transcript, 2026-10-02). Scope confirmed by Shane: only the cards
where an out-of-combat use reasonably makes sense get a reverse side, not every card. Weapon and
Equipment Cards are out of scope.

## What the Doc says (v0.7, unchanged since the 2026-09-28 draft)

- Part III "Abilities Outside Combat": a Combat Card *may* include an explicit out-of-combat entry
  on its reverse side. That entry defines what the technique can accomplish during Exploration or
  social play, "including its time, Resource, target, and other requirements". Knowing a Style
  grants no freeform effects.
- Part V "Combat Cards Have Explicit Out-of-Combat Uses": the entry is the authoritative
  description. Follow its printed time, Resource, equipment, access and other requirements.
  Ordinary Non-Combat rolls generated by that use do not gain Surges unless the entry says so.
- Part V "Utility Use Is Not Free Pre-Combat Setup": using the reverse does not start Initiative;
  using the Combat-side procedure to pre-buff does.
- Part VI "Out-of-Combat Combat Card Uses": same rule for social play. A technique can create
  leverage, reveal information, demonstrate capability, alter an environment, or communicate.
- Part II Step 7 (new wording): when choosing your 10 cards, "review their backside for potential
  out-of-combat uses, as that may alter your decisions".

## What the call adds (intent)

- The reverse is first a **scoping tool**: it tells players what to try and tells the GM how much
  is too much for a card of that Rank, so adjudication is not on the fly. Rank 0 gravity pushes a
  lever or a box; Rank 2 lifts a truck; Rank 5 fire-sword melts titanium.
- It is **mostly descriptive**: "here are ideas on how to use it". But the **cost still stands**:
  the Resource cost is always paid, and the dice commitment is honored when the scene is timed
  (chases, trap rooms, escape rooms, anything with an Action allowance per interval). Otherwise
  the dice are irrelevant and only the Resource and any printed time are paid.
- Ryan wants it on the same card as a second face ("Non-combat applications").

## Current state in the module

- `EssenceCardData` (`module/data/item-card.mjs`) already has a `flavor` HTMLField, shown on the
  card sheet in view mode and used as a summary fallback. It is empty on 315 of 319 action cards
  and all 110 reaction cards. The chat card never shows it.
- There is no notion of a card back anywhere. `nonCombatTask` (Perform Task) is the only
  non-combat routing, and it rolls Attribute + Non-Combat Skill through the non-combat engine.
- Authoring is CSV in `scripts/combat-cards/*.csv` -> `scripts/build-packs.mjs` -> `packs/_source`.

## Plan

### Phase 1: data

None. The existing `flavor` HTMLField on `EssenceCardData` is the back face. A card with empty
flavor is single-sided. Shane, 2026-10-03: "The reverse block is the flavor text, that is what
the Flavor text field is for."

### Phase 2: authoring

The `flavor` column already exists in the six combat-card CSVs, build-packs, bulk import, the card
sheet editor and the Content Wizard. Write the back as HTML in that column: a summary line, a
bullet list of concrete uses, and a "not at this Rank" line. A per-Rank scale table is written
first with Ryan and kept in `design/`.

### Phase 3: showing the flip side

Foundry has no built-in two-faced Item, but a CSS 3D flip is straightforward and works in the
item sheet, the chat card and the compendium view:

- Card sheet view mode: wrap `.card-view` in a flip container (`transform-style: preserve-3d`,
  `rotateY(180deg)` on `.flipped`). A "Flip" button in the header; only rendered when
  flavor is non-empty. The back face carries the band, name, Rank chip, the Commit and Cost
  tiles, then the flavor HTML as "Non-combat applications". Single-sided cards show no button.
- Chat card: a small "Back" toggle beside the existing Card button, flipping the posted card.
- Actor sheet card list: a reverse icon on cards that have a back; clicking it opens the sheet
  already flipped.
- Foundry's `Cards` document (decks with faces and backs) is the other option. It is built for
  dealing decks, not owned Items with rolls, so it is not used here.

### Phase 4: mechanics ("Use outside Combat")

A second play path, reached from the back face's button or a "Use outside Combat" option in the
existing Play prompt:

1. Cooldown and uses are checked as for the front (per-Encounter reads as per-scene).
2. Resource cost: `reverse.resource` ("same" pays `cost` from the domain Resource).
3. Dice: `reverse.dice` decides whether the Pool commitment is charged. "timed" asks the GM a
   one-click "Timed scene? Spend Action dice" (default off outside Combat, on when the actor is in
   an active Combat).
4. Roll: "none" posts the back face to chat with costs paid; "task" routes through the non-combat
   engine (Attribute + Non-Combat Skill, Success Dice asked, no Surges unless `reverse.surges`);
   "card" rolls the card's Attribute + Style Rank against a GM Difficulty, no Surges by default.
5. The chat card is the back face with a "paid" line (Resource, dice if charged, time).

### Phase 5: content

Author reverse entries only for cards where a use makes sense, Style by Style, using the Rank
scale table. Seed candidates: utility, movement, information, environment and buff subtypes.
Pure damage cards stay single-sided unless the effect has an obvious physical application
(cut, melt, push, light).

### Not changed

Front-face play, costs, the roll engine, Equipment Cards, and all existing pack data.
